#Empowering India Through Education & Employment

## 📊 Excel Data Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyse the relationship between **education and employment indicators across Indian states** using data from the **Economic Survey 2022-23 Statistical Appendix**.

The project transforms raw statistical data into meaningful visual insights using **Microsoft Excel, data cleaning, analysis, KPIs, and data visualisation**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand India's education and employment landscape by analysing:

- Higher Education Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)
- Literacy Rate
- Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR)
- Worker Population Ratio (WPR)
- Unemployment Rate
- State-level education performance
- Education and employment relationships

The dashboard helps users identify **state-wise differences, trends, rankings, and relationships** between education and employment indicators.

---

## 📌 Key Questions

This dashboard attempts to answer the following questions:

1. Which states have the highest Higher Education GER?
2. What is the literacy rate across Indian states?
3. How has India's employment situation changed over time?
4. Which states have the highest and lowest literacy rates?
5. Is there a relationship between literacy and labour-force participation?
6. How do education indicators vary across states?
7. What patterns can be observed between education and employment?

## 📊 Dashboard Components

The final dashboard contains **four major KPI indicators and multiple visualisations**.

### 🔹 KPI Cards

- **Higher Education GER**
- **Literacy Rate 2011**
- **Labour Force Participation**
- **Unemployment Rate**

### 🔹 Charts

#### 1. Top 10 States – Higher Education GER

Compares the top-performing states based on Higher Education Gross Enrolment Ratio.

#### 2. All-India Employment Trend

Shows the trend of:

- Labour Force Participation Rate
- Worker Population Ratio
- Unemployment Rate

#### 3. Literacy Rate – Top 8 & Bottom 8 States

Compares states with the highest and lowest literacy rates.

#### 4. Literacy Rate vs Labour Force Participation

A scatter plot used to examine the relationship between literacy and labour-force participation.
